TableFlow support

Help with installation, exports, files, billing questions and suspected defects.

Contact support

Email contact@toosolv.com. Requests may be submitted at any time. Every email receives an automatic acknowledgement so you know it arrived.

For defects and feature requests that contain no confidential information, you can also use the TableFlow issue tracker. The tracker gives every report a visible status and history.

Support hours and response target

  • Business hours: Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 Europe/Berlin time, excluding public holidays in Hamburg, Germany.
  • First personal response: within 3 business days, usually sooner.
  • Critical incidents involving data loss, a security issue or complete loss of the export function are handled first.
  • Resolution time depends on the cause and is not guaranteed. We keep you informed when investigation or a fix takes longer.

What to include

  • Your Confluence Cloud version is not needed. Tell us which export option you selected: Excel or CSV, and Single or ZIP.
  • Describe what you expected, what happened and the steps that reproduce the problem.
  • Include the error reference shown by TableFlow, if there is one.
  • Do not send table contents, exported files, passwords, tokens or other confidential information unless we specifically agree on a safe way to receive it.

Security reports

Please follow the security policy. Do not place vulnerability details in a public issue.

Platform incidents

TableFlow runs on Atlassian Forge. Check Atlassian Developer Status for an active Forge or Marketplace incident.
