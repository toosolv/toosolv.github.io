User guide

Export tables from Confluence to Excel or CSV in three steps.

Export tables in three steps

  1. Open a Confluence page that contains tables. Tables inside expand sections and page layouts are found too.
  2. Select ••• (top right) → Apps → Export tables (Excel/CSV).
  3. Check the preview, then select the Export button at the bottom right.

Excel

  • All tables are selected by default. Uncheck the circle on a table to leave it out.
  • The file contains one sheet per table. The sheet name comes from the title of the expand section or the heading above the table. The characters [ ] : * ? / \ are replaced, names are shortened to 31 characters (the Excel limit), and duplicate names get (2), (3) and so on. Tables without a name are called "Table 1", "Table 2" and so on, in your Confluence language.
  • Merged cells stay merged. Line breaks, special characters and emoji are kept.
  • Formulas are never executed: a cell like =SUM(A1:A2) is exported as text.
  • Numbers become real Excel numbers in the format of your Confluence language: in German for example 1,5, 1.234,56 and 12,5 %, in English 1,234.56. The decimals you see are kept. A column is converted only if all its cells below the header are numbers, so a column with text or references like "3,4" in a sentence stays text.
  • These values stay text: leading zeros (00123), -0, whole numbers with more than 11 digits (such as article or account numbers), currencies, dates and values like 1.234 or 1,234, which mean different things in different languages.

CSV

  • Switch the format to CSV. The table in the preview is exported.
  • Separator: ; is what Excel expects in most European languages, , is the international standard. The app picks the right one for your Confluence language, and you can change it.
  • CSV files are saved as UTF-8 so special characters open correctly in Excel.
  • Cells that start with =, +, -, @, |, a tab or a carriage return, or with the full-width forms of these characters, get an apostrophe in front, so spreadsheet programs do not treat them as formulas. Plain numbers like -5 are not changed.
  • CSV cannot store merged cells: the text of a merged cell appears in its first cell.

Limits

  • The preview shows the first 50 rows and 40 columns of a table. The exported file always contains all rows and columns.
  • An Excel cell holds at most 32,767 characters. Longer cells are cut in the Excel file. Use CSV for tables with very long cells.
  • Pages whose tables have more than 2 million cells in total cannot be exported.
  • A cell that spans several rows ends at the end of its table section (header or body), just as the browser shows it.
  • Tables that macros load later, such as Jira issue lists, Table Filter views or Confluence databases, are not part of the page content and are not exported. The app shows a note when it finds such macros.
  • The app exports the last published version of the page. Unpublished changes in the editor are not included.
  • Cells keep their text only: links become their link text, images and colours are not exported.

Languages

The app follows your Confluence language. Supported languages: English, German, French, Spanish and Japanese.

Troubleshooting

You seeWhat to do
"No tables on this page"Only real Confluence tables are exported. Tables inside images, attachments or embedded files cannot be read, and tables loaded by macros (for example Jira issue lists) are not included.
"Could not read this page"You may not have permission to view the page, or Confluence is temporarily unavailable. Select Try again.
"License not active"Ask your Confluence administrator to renew the app.
The menu item is missingThe app license may be inactive, or the app is not installed on this site.
The download does not startAllow downloads for your Confluence site in your browser. If the app shows a Save file button (for example in Safari), select it to start the download.

Privacy

The page is read in your browser with your own permissions. Nothing is sent to or stored by the vendor, and the app stores nothing in your browser. See the privacy policy.
