Summary

Page and table content stays in your browser. Table cells and generated files are not stored. Atlassian Forge keeps the newest 200 export-history metadata entries per account; older entries are removed automatically, and you can clear the history at any time. TooSolv Studio operates no external server for the app.

What the app does with your data

  • When you open "Export tables" on a Confluence page, the app reads that page's content through Atlassian's API with your own permissions. It can only read what you can already see.
  • The tables are turned into an Excel or CSV file inside your browser and downloaded to your device.
  • No page content or personal data is sent to TooSolv Studio or to an external analytics or hosting service. History remains inside Atlassian Forge. TooSolv Studio operates no servers for this app. The only thing we receive is the counters described below under "Cookies, tracking, analytics".
  • For the export history, Atlassian Forge stores the date and time, downloaded file name, export format and delivery mode, included table names, the Confluence page identifier and stable Confluence table identifiers when available. The identifiers prevent "Repeat export" from reading a different page or silently choosing another table after a page edit. The history is separated by app installation and signed-in Atlassian account.
  • If an error occurs, the app shows a short diagnostic line: an error code, the app version, the identifier of the Confluence page and your language setting. It offers to copy that line to your clipboard. Whether you send it to us is your decision. If you do, we handle it like any other support message.
  • If you select Help in an error message, your browser opens our documentation page on this website. Nothing about your Confluence page is sent with it. What the web host logs is described under "This website" below.
  • Forge gives app vendors access to server-side app logs for sites where log sharing is enabled. TableCrate does not write page content, table content, file names or export-history records to those logs. Technical errors can appear in your browser console for troubleshooting, but the app does not send that browser-console output to TooSolv Studio. Your administrator can switch Forge log sharing off at any time at admin.atlassian.com under Apps, Connected apps.

Data stored in your browser and Atlassian Forge

The app stores your quick-start completion, app-language choice, display-theme choice, Excel number-format choice and CSV-separator choice in local browser storage. Forge keeps the newest 200 export-history metadata entries per account: export date and time, file name, format and delivery mode, included table names, the source page identifier and stable Confluence table identifiers when available. Older entries are removed automatically. A one-way hash of your Atlassian account identifier keeps each account's history separate. Neither table-cell content nor an exported file is stored. During migration or a temporary Forge failure, unsent history metadata can remain in the browser until Forge accepts it. The app sets no cookies. The exported file is saved to your device only when you select Export. The clipboard is used only when you select "Copy details" yourself.

A separate Forge privacy record keeps your Atlassian account identifier and its reporting timestamp solely for Atlassian's Privacy API. A weekly Forge task reports accounts for which export history exists. If Atlassian reports that an account has been closed, TableCrate deletes that account's history and privacy record. Selecting Clear history also removes the related privacy record once deletion is complete.

Retention and deletion

Forge retains up to the newest 200 export-history entries for your account. Adding another entry automatically removes the oldest entry above that limit. Clearing the history removes every remaining history record for your account in that app installation; files already downloaded to your device remain unchanged. If the app is uninstalled, Atlassian retains Forge hosted app data for 28 days under its platform lifecycle and then removes it. TooSolv Studio keeps no separate copy.

Atlassian

The app is hosted and run by Atlassian as part of Confluence Cloud. Its history uses Atlassian Forge hosted storage. The storage location follows the data-residency realm selected for the customer's Atlassian site. If no realm is pinned, Atlassian may host in its global realms; if a realm such as Germany, the EU, the USA or another supported location is pinned, Forge hosted storage follows that choice. TooSolv Studio does not choose or operate a separate storage country. Atlassian's processing of your data is governed by Atlassian's own privacy policy.

Cookies, tracking, analytics

The app sets no cookies and does not track you. It does keep eight simple counters, one for each of these events, so that we can tell whether the app is working for our customers:

  • a page was read and had tables
  • a page was read and had no tables
  • opening the page did not work
  • an Excel file was created
  • a CSV file was created
  • creating a file did not work
  • a table was too large to export
  • the app hit an unexpected error and showed you a failure message

That is the whole list. What the app sends is a number and nothing else: no page content, no table or file names, no page or space identifiers, no error text, and nothing about who you are.

To be precise about the rest: the counters are collected by Atlassian's Forge platform, and the platform itself adds the site the app is installed on, the app version and the time. So in Atlassian's developer console we can see that a site exported something, and roughly when, but never what, from which page, or by whom. Atlassian shows us these counts for the last 14 days only. We consider them non-personal data, because nothing in them points to an individual; should they ever be regarded as personal data in your case, our legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in knowing whether the software we sell is working.

Your Confluence administrator can switch the counters off for your whole Atlassian site at any time: go to admin.atlassian.com, select the site, select Apps, then Connected apps, choose this app, and in the Details tab turn off "Custom metrics access". The app works exactly the same either way.
