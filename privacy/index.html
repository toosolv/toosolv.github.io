Privacy policy

Who we are

Table Export for Confluence ("the app") is provided by TooSolv Studio, Ryan-Jamil Rezgaoui (postal address in our legal notice). Contact: contact@toosolv.com

Summary

The app does not collect, store or transfer any personal data or page content. It runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform inside your web browser.

What the app does with your data

  • When you open "Export tables" on a Confluence page or blog post, the app reads that page's content through Atlassian's API with your own permissions. It can only read what you can already see.
  • The tables are turned into an Excel or CSV file inside your browser and downloaded to your device.
  • No page content, personal data or usage data is sent to TooSolv Studio or to any third party. TooSolv Studio operates no servers for this app.

Data stored in your browser

The app keeps two small values in your browser's local storage: the number of exports you made and whether you dismissed the one-time review request. They contain no page content and no personal data, stay on your device and can be deleted by clearing your browser's site data.

Atlassian

The app is hosted and run by Atlassian as part of Confluence Cloud. Atlassian's processing of your data is governed by Atlassian's own privacy policy.

Cookies, tracking, analytics

The app sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics.

These can contain the name of your organization and the name and e-mail address of the billing and technical contacts. We use this data only to manage licenses, answer questions and meet our accounting duties. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract) and Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (legal retention duties). We keep it for as long as the license exists and afterwards for as long as commercial and tax law requires (up to 10 years).
